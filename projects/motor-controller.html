DC Motor Controller

My lab team is designing a DC motor controller from scratch in digital logic, built in Quartus Prime and run on FPGA hardware. I own the state machine and direction control, and I lead testing on the board.

What it is and why I did it

The goal of the lab is to control a DC motor using only digital logic we design ourselves, with no pre-built motor controller chip. It ties together everything from the course: combinational logic, sequential logic, and state machines.

My role

I designed the control state machine and the direction-control logic, and I handle testing on the FPGA board.

Process

  1. Defined the controller’s states and the transitions between them.
  2. Built the direction-control logic that sets which way the motor spins.
  3. Entered and simulated the design in Quartus Prime before putting it on hardware.
  4. Programmed the FPGA board and tested the motor’s response, then debugged against the simulation.
